How Predicia works (the math!)

Predicia Engine v3.1 · Powered by Ensemble Forecasting + Monte Carlo Simulation + Multi-Factor Signal Analysis + Sentiment Aggregation

These are the same kinds of math techniques used by Wall Street's biggest hedge funds (like Renaissance Technologies and Two Sigma) and by Nobel Prize winners like Eugene Fama and Harry Markowitz who invented modern finance. The idea: combine LOTS of different signals together — that's called an ensemble. No single signal is perfect, but if 10 signals all agree, you can be much more confident than relying on any one.

Predicia mixes 10 different signals. Each signal becomes a number from -1 (very bad sign) to +1 (very good sign), and they get added up with different weights to make the final Predicia Score:

  1. Momentum (14%) — Has the price been speeding up recently? Compares the last 5 days to the 25 days before.
  2. Trend (13%) — Is the price above its 20-day average? Real traders use this all the time.
  3. News (13%) — Are recent events good or bad for the company? Each event gets a positive or negative tag.
  4. Analyst rating (12%) — What do Wall Street experts recommend? Strong Buy → Strong Sell.
  5. Sector strength (11%) — How are OTHER stocks in the same sector doing? When all gaming stocks rise, that helps a gaming company.
  6. Range position (10%) — Where is the price in its 30-day high-low range? Near the top is strong.
  7. MA Cross (9%) — Is the 5-day average above the 20-day average? When this flips up it's called a "Golden Cross" — bullish!
  8. Streak (8%) — Has the price gone up (or down) several days in a row? Streaks have momentum.
  9. Social buzz (5%) — Are people searching for and talking about this company online?
  10. Calmness (5%) — How bumpy is the stock? Smoother stocks get more confident predictions.
Predicia Score = Σ (weight × signal)
verdict = UP if score > 0.1, DOWN if score < -0.1, MIXED otherwise
confidence boosted when many signals agree (up to 94%)

More signals + more agreement = higher confidence. If 9 out of 10 signals all point the same way, Predicia is much surer. If signals fight each other, confidence drops. Predicia never says more than 94% sure — because the stock market is genuinely unpredictable.
